About Us

Welcome to Mahadev Dana Pani

Welcome to Mahadev Dana Pani, a vegetarian restaurant serving delicious North Indian food in Muzaffarnagar. Whether you're looking for a satisfying meal, a quick bite or a comfortable place to enjoy traditional flavours, Mahadev Dana Pani brings hearty food and warm hospitality together.

Located right by the Roadways Bus Stand, we're an easy stop for travellers and a regular favourite for local families — serving simple, honest vegetarian food made the way it should be.
